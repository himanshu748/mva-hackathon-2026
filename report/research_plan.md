# Research plan: selective clearance with preserved tissue function

Updated 11 September 2026. Proposed preclinical work; no new biological experiments have been performed by this team. Model access, a qualified laboratory team and an experimental budget are not secured.

## Question and decision

Can dasatinib alone improve a tissue deficit in a BUB1B disease model through selective removal of a persistent senescent population, at an exposure that preserves developing and repair-capable cells?

Retain dasatinib as a conditional clearance nomination; the evidence does not establish priority over preservation candidates. Let the measured defect determine the experimental branch. Evidence for altered cell state, a lower marker or a more favorable cell proportion does not by itself demonstrate clearance, restored chromosome segregation or tissue benefit. The report and evidence ledger distinguish the same-gene genetic experiment, patent-reported pharmacology, adult human studies and our exploratory public mouse-data audit.

## Model and target gate

Use a disease-background model, independently verified correction/complementation controls and unrelated controls. Verify genotype and relevant allele effects before treating the model as a representation of the disease. A patient-derived source would require consent and institutional authorization; access is not assumed.

Measure allele-resolved RNA, full-length/truncated BUBR1 abundance and residual function after establishing phase. The submitted c.3006T>G allele must not inherit a database classification from c.3006T>A merely because both encode p.Asn1002Lys. The [patient-benefit review](patient_benefit_review.md) records exact-allele checks and restoration evidence.

Record passage, population doublings, oxygen conditions, differentiation state and cell-line identity across conditions. Include quiescent and differentiated nonsenescent controls as well as proliferating cells and an assay-positive senescence control. Require complementary arrest, senescence-associated and secretory readouts; one marker does not define the target. Reprogramming can reset cell state, so a pluripotent derivative cannot automatically stand in for the donor's original senescence burden. [Lapasset 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22056670/).

If the hypothesized persistent target cannot be demonstrated in the chosen model, do not advance the clearance explanation there. An induced-senescence assay control can validate detection but cannot supply the missing disease phenotype. Distinguish persistent harmful states from transient reparative states; the latter supported wound closure in a primary mouse study. [Demaria 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2014.11.012).

## Exposure gate

Produce a pharmacology record before interpreting an active concentration as useful: compound/product identity, chemical form, formulation, solvent, measured medium concentration and stability, exposure duration and washout, binding/free-exposure assumptions and relevant metabolites. Compare active exposure with documented exposure in a relevant compartment; identify rather than fill gaps in tissue pharmacokinetics.

Compare useful target activity and neural differentiation/function across matched concentration-time conditions, with measured or explicitly justified unbound exposure. Published senolytic and neural-harm experiments overlap in nominal dasatinib concentrations but differ in model, medium and duration. They cannot supply a numerical safety margin. [Zhu 2015](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344), [Lombardo 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843680/).

An oral dose, a nominal culture concentration and measured tissue exposure are different quantities. Total plasma peaks, fixed protein-binding assumptions, intermittent administration and viability alone cannot establish a useful neural exposure window. Reject a translational inference that depends on an implausible exposure, even if a culture result appears selective. Product-specific absorption and interactions need review. These records are not a pediatric regimen.

## Comparison and causal attribution

If persistent harmful senescence is established, compare vehicle with dasatinib alone under matched conditions. Quercetin and D+Q are not nominated drugs or combinations in this proposal; their published experiments remain indirect background evidence. Report the same endpoint and time point for every arm. Select a separate preservation comparator from the demonstrated defect: sodium phenylbutyrate for protein stress or metformin for an oligodendrocyte differentiation/myelination deficit. Assess each preservation comparator independently against its matched control; no combination is nominated.

The approved candidate must demonstrate its own activity: results from a published combination cannot substitute for a dasatinib-only comparison. An ATP-based viability signal alone does not establish selective death. Pair absolute cell counts and direct fate measurements with functional outcomes.

Sodium phenylbutyrate is a named comparison because the drug reduced aggregate and apoptosis readouts during human trisomic neural induction. This does not establish recovery after a functional deficit or benefit in ongoing BUB1B-related chromosome instability. [Hirata 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-70362-x). Distinguish treatment during differentiation from delayed treatment after a measured deficit, with each arm compared against time-matched controls. Require total neural functional output and durability, not only aggregate reduction, survival or cell number.

The parent compound and its phenylacetate metabolite must both enter the pharmacology assessment. The current label warns about phenylacetate neurotoxicity and reports developmental neural harm in juvenile animals. Approved use in urea-cycle disorders does not establish a neuroprotective exposure in MVA. [PHEBURANE label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d15f2cbf-4d22-40b5-8ef9-16b9d323a7f9).

Genetic rescue in a fly neural model further motivates measuring mitochondrial/proteostasis impairment. It does not establish that an approved drug reproduces that intervention. [González-Blanco 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-70521-0).

Genotype correction addresses disease causality. To assess whether drug-associated functional benefit is mediated by cell clearance, use an orthogonal selective-clearance comparison where feasible. If this is unavailable, report mediation as unresolved. Genetic loss of p16 changes checkpoint biology and is not an interchangeable cell-clearance control.

## Outcomes and decisions

The 2026 SM/J disc study reports cell retention alongside lower degeneration after D+Q and proposes a preservation mechanism. This is a different intervention and model, but it strengthens the need to distinguish clearance, suppressed secretion and retained cell function in our experiment. If useful function improves without selective clearance, report the mechanism as unresolved or preservation-associated rather than declaring senolysis. [Novais 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41413-026-00526-4).

Residual BUBR1/checkpoint activity must also be assessed when surviving cells resume division after washout. Dasatinib caused reversible growth arrest and reduced cell-cycle transcripts, including BUB1B, in a separate non-MVA fibroblast study. These results do not prove checkpoint injury, but they prevent treating reduced proliferation or marker expression as evidence of selective clearance. [Haubeiss 2010](https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-4598-9-168), including supplementary Table S2.

| Domain | Record | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Complementary measures of persistent arrest, senescence state and secretion | Absent target stops this mechanism in the tested model |
| Selective activity | Absolute initial/final cell counts and fate/death in target and nontarget populations | Nonspecific killing rejects selectivity; marker suppression is not clearance |
| Tissue function | A prespecified model-relevant functional outcome; total output and output per surviving cell | Net loss cannot be hidden by enrichment of healthier survivors |
| Neural function | Differentiation, process complexity/myelin-related outcomes and feasible axonal support/function measures | Dysfunction blocks advancement at the tested exposure even with preserved viability |
| Chromosome errors | Errors per observed division in survivors, total cell yield and aneuploid fractions | A favorable fraction cannot be called checkpoint repair; worsened errors block advancement |
| Durability | Target recurrence, survivor behavior and function after washout | Immediate changes do not establish durable benefit |
| Growth, repair, marrow and cancer context | Healthy-tissue capacity and relevant tumor/non-tumor outcomes | Adverse net effects block translation; interaction with oncology care is separate |

A candidate functional endpoint is contractile output in a validated muscle model. Its suitability and measurement variance must be established in feasibility work. Neural differentiation/myelin measurements complement a tissue endpoint and can veto an otherwise favorable result; they must not be averaged away in a composite score.

## Analysis decisions before outcome collection

The biological unit is an independently justified donor, culture preparation, animal or construct. Cells, images and technical wells are nested observations, not independent patients. Aggregate technical measurements within the chosen unit or use a justified hierarchical model. Report donor, clone, preparation, well and cell counts separately. Several clones from one donor do not establish between-patient generalizability.

Randomize treatment within batches, balance all arms, blind image scoring and adjudication, and specify exclusions/missing-data handling. A laboratory team must select one primary functional contrast, meaningful benefit and harm margins, exposure schedule, follow-up duration, replication standard and multiplicity strategy before confirmatory work. Use a separate feasibility phase to estimate variability and plan sample size. No adequate power or numerical success threshold is claimed in this proposal.

The dasatinib-only MSC study provides a concrete caution: assess total functional output alongside output normalized to surviving-cell number or DNA, distinguish serial passage from donor age, and measure cell fate rather than infer selective death from fewer cells per image. Its mixed differentiation and inconclusive cartilage results cannot support a general preservation claim. [Heinrichs et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12195-024-00830-1).

Estimate effects and uncertainty at the biological-unit level. A nonsignificant harm test does not establish preservation; use justified equivalence/noninferiority margins where appropriate. Missing evidence of harm is not interchangeable with evidence of a therapeutic window.

## Existing computational work

The companion public-data audit uses GSE134780, a mouse genotype comparison without drug arms. Its literature-informed marker panel, sample alignment, descriptive normalization, alternate-normalization comparison and sample-omission checks are reproducible. This evaluates the stability of expression descriptions, not pharmacologic response. Do not pool different GEO series as age effects or independent validation when design and metadata differ.

## Feasibility and expected research value

The current handoff contains code, data provenance, a balanced source ledger and decisions for a qualified research team. No wet-laboratory timetable, cost estimate, patient consultation or tissue access is asserted. A supported negative result is useful if the model, exposure, controls and endpoints are sufficient to distinguish missing target, lack of selectivity, inadequate exposure and tissue harm.

The reuse claim concerns the evidence structure and analysis code. A different MVA allele, gene, tissue or patient requires fresh target and safety evidence. Publish permitted findings with complete attribution and data-use compliance; do not redistribute the child's underlying dataset.
