# What would make this proposal useful to the child?

Research follow-up, 8 September 2026. This document assesses evidence and changes the proposed research questions. It does not provide a treatment plan.

## Current answer

We have not demonstrated that dasatinib, D+Q or an alternative can improve this child's health or survival. The proposed mechanism, relevant tissue response and feasible therapeutic window remain unmeasured. The hackathon result establishes agreement with its variant answer key; it cannot predict a treatment response.

This targeted review found no human study demonstrating correction of BUB1B-related MVA or a disease-modifying survival benefit. A separate reviewer searched the ClinicalTrials.gov API for the full disease name, BUB1B and premature chromatid separation on 8 September; those searches returned no studies. The parent repeated the [full-disease query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.term=%22mosaic%20variegated%20aneuploidy%22&countTotal=true&pageSize=100&format=json). An unrelated breast-cancer result from “MVA syndrome” was excluded. These searches do not establish that no relevant research or broader eligibility study exists.

Care for complications remains important. A [longitudinal case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7455400/) documents survival into adulthood with treatment of renal disease and tumors, but cannot attribute survival to a particular intervention. The [2024 AACR consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39264246/) addresses cancer surveillance in genomic instability disorders including MVA. Clinical priorities and surveillance belong with the treating genetics and specialist team; this hackathon review lacks the clinical assessment needed to individualize them.

## Match the DNA change before borrowing evidence

The submitted missense allele is **NM_001211.6:c.3006T>G**, p.Asn1002Lys. Exact coding and genomic ClinVar searches did not identify a record. [ClinVar 4600147](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/4600147/) describes **c.3006T>A**, which produces the same amino-acid change but is a different DNA variant. Its single-submitter uncertain-significance classification must not be assigned to the submitted T>G allele. An absent database match also cannot establish pathogenicity or benignity.

The exact stop allele **c.2210T>G, p.Leu737Ter** is [ClinVar 533901](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/533901/), with pathogenic/likely pathogenic assertions from multiple submitters without conflicts. Its RNA-decay/truncation consequence remains predicted in that record. Classification, measured residual function and drug responsiveness are separate evidence questions.

## More direct restoration deserves a control

Wild-type BUB1B complementation improved checkpoint responses in other MVA patient-derived fibroblast lines. Those lines were SV40-transformed. Other replacement experiments separated low protein abundance from intrinsic functional defects. This supports measuring both abundance and function; it does not show that the submitted missense allele can be rescued. [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/).

Genetic SIRT2 overexpression extended survival in BubR1 hypomorphic mice, with a male-driven effect. These animals retain low amounts of wild-type-sequence protein. The separate NMN experiment restored BubR1 abundance in aged wild-type mouse testes; it did not demonstrate NMN-mediated MVA rescue or survival extension. Increasing an unknown mutant protein's abundance cannot be assumed to restore its function. [North et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194088/).

## A closer drug comparison in human trisomic neurons

Sodium 4-phenylbutyrate (4-PBA) is worth testing as a separate protein-stress comparison. Hirata and colleagues studied neurons derived from two trisomy-21 donors and one trisomy-13 donor, with multiple clones; additional corrected controls supported the baseline trisomy-associated pathology. Treatment reduced aggregate, ER-stress and cleaved-caspase-3 readouts. It began at neural induction and continued to day 14; the main Methods reports 100 micromolar culture exposure. This is a literature concentration, not a proposed human regimen. The study did not demonstrate electrophysiological recovery or reversal of a mature-neuron deficit. Clone-level observations must not be counted as independent patients. [Hirata 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-70362-x).

Earlier work likewise prevented emerging senescence in trisomy-21-derived fibroblasts. It did not establish recovery of an existing neural deficit. [Nawa 2019](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0219592). Both studies concern constitutive trisomy; transfer to continuing BUB1B-related chromosome errors is untested. The drug has several reported actions, so reduced aggregates alone cannot prove which mechanism caused a benefit.

The July 2026 PHEBURANE label confirms sodium phenylbutyrate use in adult/pediatric urea-cycle disorders. It describes rapid conversion to phenylacetate, an adult parent-drug half-life of 0.5–0.8 hours, and neurotoxicity concerns for the metabolite. A brief plasma peak cannot stand in for repeated culture exposure or measured unbound brain exposure. [Current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d15f2cbf-4d22-40b5-8ef9-16b9d323a7f9).

This justifies a conditional comparison, with no established superiority over dasatinib. The decisive result would be durable total neural functional improvement after a deficit has appeared, at justified parent/metabolite exposures, without worsening chromosome errors or growth control. No such result has been generated here.

## Neural-preservation mechanisms and other medicines

A 2026 study depleted **bub3 or rod in Drosophila neural stem cells**. Genetic enhancement of mitochondrial chaperones or reactive-oxygen-species handling restored brain size while only partly restoring neuroblast counts. Rapamycin increased neuroblast counts; the reported genetic autophagy interventions did not restore progeny-cell numbers or brain size. These findings distinguish cell retention from tissue recovery. The study did not test this child's BUB1B alleles or demonstrate human neural function or clinical benefit. [González-Blanco et al., 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-70521-0).

This provides a reason to measure mitochondrial function, protein handling and neural differentiation in a relevant disease model before choosing a cell-preserving comparison. It does not identify an antioxidant supplement or approved medicine that reproduces the genetic intervention. Normal brain size also cannot be treated as normal chromosome segregation or neurological function.

Two approved medicines were considered as possible mitochondrial/redox comparisons:

| Medicine | Relevant evidence | Why it does not resolve MVA treatment selection |
|---|---|---|
| Elamipretide / Forzinity | FDA accelerated approval for muscle strength in Barth syndrome in 2025. The randomized portion missed walking/fatigue endpoints; the approval relied on strength changes during the uncontrolled extension. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshots-forzinity) | Cardiolipin binding in Barth syndrome does not reproduce genetic chaperone rescue in aneuploid neural cells. A separate 218-person mitochondrial-myopathy trial also missed walking/fatigue endpoints. [MMPOWER-3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37268435/) |
| Omaveloxolone / Skyclarys | FDA approval for Friedreich ataxia, age 16 or older, with randomized neurological endpoint evidence. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshots-skyclarys) | The trial concerns frataxin deficiency. It does not demonstrate BUB1B restoration or benefit in aneuploid neural precursors. Its clinical benefit in that disease does not establish an MVA therapeutic window. |

Neither has verified superiority over the current research candidate in an MVA model. The current candidate also has no verified superiority over these alternatives. A clinical drug switch is unsupported by this comparison.

## Changes to the proposed work

The first useful measurement is allele-resolved RNA and BUBR1 protein/function in an appropriately authorized disease model, with phase established and independently verified correction/complementation controls. Determine whether residual protein is absent, unstable but functional, or intrinsically impaired.

Then compare persistent senescence with mitochondrial/proteostasis impairment and loss of differentiation. Dasatinib has no automatic priority. Sodium phenylbutyrate is the named protein-stress comparison; metformin remains conditional on an oligodendrocyte differentiation/myelination deficit. Choose the branch only when a measured defect supports its mechanism. Require improved total tissue function and appropriate differentiation, with absolute cell-fate counts and durability. Measure ongoing chromosome-segregation errors and reject worsening. Retaining abnormal cells, suppressing a marker or enlarging a tissue is insufficient.

These measurements require a qualified laboratory and clinical interpretation. Patient material, partners and funding remain unsecured. The immediate deliverable is a testable research handoff that makes an ineffective or harmful candidate easier to reject.
