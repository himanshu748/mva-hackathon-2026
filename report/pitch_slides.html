Mechanism

BUB1B null + hypomorph
→ reduced BUBR1 dosage
→ spindle assembly checkpoint slips
→ chromosomes missegregate (the variegated aneuploidy)
→ aneuploidy stress and DNA damage
→ senescent cells accumulate + SASP
→ tissue failure, growth restriction, tumour-promoting niche

You cannot drug a missing allele. You can drug that.

The problem with our own proposal

Dasatinib affects growth in children.
This child's presenting problem
is growth failure.

  • Our claim: intermittent dosing changes the exposure profile.
  • Our evidence: none yet. That is experiment 5.
  • Kill switch: if patient cells show no senescent burden, the hypothesis is dead.
